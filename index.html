01
Figures, cards, character goods

AKIHABARA RADIO KAIKAN

秋葉原ラジオ会館

Glass cases of figures on every floor, from Dragon Ball Z and Evangelion to Demon Slayer and Jujutsu Kaisen. Look into the small shops and see what you can find. The photo shows a figure case in an Akihabara shop.

02
Character clothes and goods

COSPA G-STORE AKIBA, 4TH FLOOR

コスパ ジーストア・アキバ店

Character apparel and goods on the 4th floor of the Onoden main building, a few minutes from Radio Kaikan. Take the lift to the 4th floor. The photo shows the building.

03
Arcade and crane games

GIGO AKIHABARA 3

GiGO 秋葉原3号館

Rows and rows of crane games filled with anime prizes. Try a few machines and win something to take home. You do not need to win to enjoy it.

Google Maps →
04
Figures and toys, packed to the ceiling

TRADER

トレーダー

A bright shop crammed with figures and toys. Take your time and look at every shelf: this is where Carolina can hunt for Mazinger Z. The photo shows a case of vintage robot toys, the kind of thing we look for.

Google Maps →
05
Anime goods, many series

ANIMATE AKIHABARA

アニメイト秋葉原

Several floors of current anime and manga goods: Demon Slayer, SPY×FAMILY, Jujutsu Kaisen, HUNTER×HUNTER, Dandadan and more. The photo shows the shop front on Chuo-dori. A good place for souvenirs.

10:00 to 21:00

10:00 to 21:00, open daily (official site, checked on 5 Nov 2026)

Google Maps →
06
Used manga, figures and games

BOOKOFF AKIHABARA EKIMAE

ブックオフ 秋葉原駅前店

A treasure hunt for second-hand manga: long shelves of Dragon Ball, Naruto, Jujutsu Kaisen and more, plus used figures and games at small prices. It is right by the station, so it is a good last stop. The photo shows a manga shop in Japan, the kind of shelf we look at.
