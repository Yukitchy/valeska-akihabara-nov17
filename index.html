Walk 3 min to Radio Kaikan

211:10

Radio Kaikan, figures

A landmark building packed with figure and hobby shops, floor after floor. A great place to find Dragon Ball Z and Evangelion figures, and we can look for Demon Slayer and Jujutsu Kaisen too.

  • Radio Kaikan on Google Maps

Next

Walk about 8 min to Mandarake

312:00

Mandarake Complex

Mandarake is a famous second-hand shop for manga, figures and collectibles. Look for old manga volumes, vintage toys and retro anime goods: Rurouni Kenshin, Dragon Ball, and for Carolina, Mazinger Z. It opens at 12:00, so we arrive right on time.

Walk 2 min to Super Potato

412:40

Super Potato, retro games

Retro games from the Famicom, Super Famicom and beyond, over several floors of the Kitabayashi Building. There is also an arcade on the top floor, so we can see classic games, including Dragon Ball Z games, if they have them.

11:00 to 20:00

Walk about 5 min to Animate

513:10

Animate Akihabara

Animate is Japan's biggest anime shop chain. This is where to look for current goods and apparel: Demon Slayer, SPY×FAMILY, Jujutsu Kaisen, HUNTER×HUNTER, Dandadan and more. A good stop for souvenirs.

  • Animate on Google Maps
  • Official site

Next

Walk about 6 min to Kanda Myojin

613:35

Kanda Myojin, and done

A shrine just up the hill from Akihabara, loved by anime fans. It has appeared in anime such as Love Live! and Steins;Gate, and it is known for charms for electronics and IT. A calm end to the day.

From here it is about 15 min on foot back to JR Akihabara Station, where we say goodbye around 14:00.
